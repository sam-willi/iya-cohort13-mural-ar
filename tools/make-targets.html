Make AR targets from a photo of the wall

Turns one straight-on photo of the painted wall into everything the camera needs to recognise it: the straightened picture, one image per AR target, the compiled targets/targets.mind, and the matching data/objects.json. Target boxes come from targets in objects.json.

Straightened wall, with the panel grid and AR targets

Appears once the corners are marked.

The white grid should sit exactly on the panel seams. Red boxes are the AR targets, blue ones the windows.

1. Photo

  • Stand far back and zoom in (2× lens) so the wall's edges stay straight. Use the camera's highest-resolution setting.
  • Get the whole painted area in, as square-on as you can, in even light with no glare.

2. Corners

Drag the four blue dots onto the corners of the painted area. The circle below zooms in on the dot you're moving.

3. Checks

  • Loading data/objects.json…

4. Build and save

Saving into the folder works in Chrome or Edge: pick the project folder (the one with index.html in it) and allow editing. In other browsers, download the .zip and unzip it over the project folder.
